Why do we need another matching service?

Because I think we can improve on what currently exists.

LoTW has high usage but that usage falls off outside of the US due to no localization of the web interface and low localization of the documentation. It's also largely stagnant due to lack of development, and recent communication from the ARRL shows that they view competing award schemes that use LoTW confirmations as competitors and freeriders. First-mover advantage (21 years of active use) and a prestigious award scheme that uses the confirmations (DXCC) has kept the usage sustained.

QRZ also does contact matching but to my knowledge only its own awards scheme is built on top of them.

Club Log does contact matching but the goal of its matching is to show contacts that would lead to new confirmed grids, countries, etc. if confirmed, as well as facilitate confirmation payments (primarily for those in rare locations). It does not have its own awards scheme.

eQSL also has some usage and its own small awards scheme but its technology is dated.

None of the above options have their source code available and so cannot benefit from outside help. LoTW, QRZ, and Club Log do contact matching as a small part of their overall work.

RGMS is focused on doing one thing well and being cost-efficient to run.

How can I help?

If you're a programmer, the source for this website is on GitHub. It's written in Python and Django. The ADIF matcher is written in Go.

If you can help localize this website into another language, please reach out to translation@radio.games.
